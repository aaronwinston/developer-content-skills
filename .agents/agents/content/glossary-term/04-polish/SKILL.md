## Glossary style

- Short, direct sentences; practitioner tone.
- Expand acronyms on first use.
- Key takeaway bullets should each deliver a concrete reader outcome.
- Headings in title case per [formatting](../../../../../references/content/formatting.md).
- Hyphens, not en dashes, unless house style explicitly requires otherwise.

## Global rules

0. Accuracy: no invented metrics, customers, or integrations.
1. Structure: one H1, logical H2/H3, FAQ at the end; no orphan headings.
2. Length: stay in the ~700–1,400 word band unless the user asks otherwise.
3. Lists: bullets for takeaways and grouped ideas; numbered lists only for sequences.
4. Competitors: no competitor names, links, or comparison framing.
5. Anchor text: descriptive, phrase-level where possible.
6. Links: at least five distinct `https://arize.com` destinations remain after edits.
7. Statistics: any number still in the draft must remain tied to a credible linked source; strip or rephrase if not.

## Checklist before handoff

- [ ] Key Takeaways + FAQ present and usable
- [ ] Product terms match brand lexicon
