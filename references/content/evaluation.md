# The on-brand checklist

> Example pre-publish checklist. Replace "messaging themes" and audience tests with yours.

Before you publish. One minute, ten questions. If you answer "no" to any of them, rewrite that section.

1. Does the first paragraph lead with a specific claim, number, tension, or story, not a generic industry setup?
2. Could a senior AI engineer read this and believe the author has actually shipped an agent?
3. Have I named the specific tools, statuses, numbers, or customers that ground the claims?
4. Have I used first-person plural ("we built," "we learned") anywhere it is the honest choice?
5. Have I cut every instance of unlock, empower, leverage, revolutionary, cutting-edge, next-generation, seamless, robust, and transformative?
6. Is there a moment where I admit something was hard, took multiple tries, or is not fully solved?
7. If I mention a competitor, is the tone measured, differentiating on philosophy and architecture rather than dunking?
8. Does at least one section connect back to one of our six messaging themes?
9. Does the piece end with something the reader can act on, such as a playbook, doc link, or next step?
10. Is the tagline, positioning, or product name used consistently with this guide?
